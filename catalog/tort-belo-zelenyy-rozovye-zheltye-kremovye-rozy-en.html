Description

White-green cake with pink and yellow cream roses — A delicate celebration cake with white cream and a soft green drip, decorated with pink and yellow cream roses and sprigs of gypsophila. Serves about 5 people. Delivered in a nice window box with candles, plates and forks included. The cake can be ordered online only together with a bouquet, balloons or a gift set. We deliver the cake to Premier Havana and other hotels within Nha Trang, Bãi Dài and northern Cam Ranh. The airport and locations south of it are outside our delivery area. We normally hand the order to reception.

Delivery & payment

  • Free delivery across Nha Trang
  • Online delivery from the next day, with slots from 06:00 to 21:00
  • Southern suburbs +300,000 VND; Bãi Dài/northern Cam Ranh +600,000 VND; airport and locations south of it excluded
  • Payment: VND, USD, RUB, USDT, cash

FAQ

Q Can you deliver a cake to a hotel?

Yes, we deliver cakes to hotels in Nha Trang and Cam Ranh, including Premier Havana. By default we leave the order at the front desk; if the lift works without a keycard, we bring it up to your room.

Q When is the earliest delivery?

Online delivery is available from the next day, with time slots from 06:00 to 21:00. Delivery within Nha Trang is free.

Q How can I pay?

We accept VND, USD, RUB, USDT and cash. Message us on WhatsApp or Telegram and we'll suggest the easiest option.
