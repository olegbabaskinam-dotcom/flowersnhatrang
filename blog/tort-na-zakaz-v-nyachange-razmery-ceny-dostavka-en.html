Custom Cakes in Nha Trang: Sizes, Prices and How to Order

Celebrating a birthday or anniversary in Nha Trang and looking for a cake? We make fresh cakes for the occasion — from a single-serving pastry to a full party cake. This guide covers the types, prices, how cakes are delivered and how to place an order.

What cakes you can order

We build cakes around your occasion: delicate cakes with cream flowers for a birthday, chocolate cakes with fruit and berries, themed cakes (with dollars, a bottle, a hobby) and small single pastries.

We design the look around your idea: colour, a Happy Birthday topper, a message, berries or cream roses. Send an example and we'll make something similar.

Sizes and three price levels

To make choosing easier, there are three levels by price and size:

  • from 100,000₫ — a small pastry, one serving;
  • from 250,000₫ — a medium small cake;
  • from 500,000₫ — a full cake, serves about 5 people.

Large combo cakes with bouquets and balloons are separate sets and cost more. We'll confirm the exact price for your size and design in chat.

How the cake is boxed and delivered

A large cake arrives in a nice window box; by default we include candles, plates and forks, so you can light up and serve right away. A pastry comes in a neat little box with a spoon.

Online orders are delivered from the next day, with slots from 06:00 to 21:00. Delivery within Nha Trang is free; we bring it to your hotel — to the front desk by default, or up to your room if the lift works without a keycard.

Key rule: a cake is an add-on

Through the online cart a cake is ordered only together with a bouquet, balloons or a gift set — we don't deliver a cake on its own. It keeps quality high and makes one complete gift rather than a trip for a single box.

If you only need a cake, message our operator and we'll suggest the easiest way.

How to order a cake in Nha Trang

Pick a cake in the catalog or send an example on WhatsApp/Telegram, add a bouquet or balloons, give the date and address (a hotel works too) and choose how to pay. We'll send a photo of the finished cake before dispatch and deliver at the agreed time.

Plan ahead: online delivery starts the next day. For an urgent delivery today, check with our operator before 18:00.
