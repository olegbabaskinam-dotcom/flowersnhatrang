Veranda restaurant in Nha Trang: a quiet countryside escape with a view of the mountains.

Author: Oksana Yasnova · 15 November 2026

Translated from the Russian original written by the author.

Veranda bar & restaurant is located in the far north of Nha Trang, among picturesque green mountains, far from the city and its bustle. The atmosphere here feels like a summer house in the countryside.

You can't get to the café on foot: this is the very edge of the city, where the intercity buses stop. The restaurant is tucked away on the outskirts, and the way to it leads along an inconspicuous path and over a small bridge across a canal.

On the left, right after the gate, is the entrance to the children's playroom. Further along the road there is a semicircular area with tables and a stage. Then comes a zone with a “GAME” sign, and after it the entrance to the restaurant. The grounds are large and green, with lots of trees and palms.

The restaurant itself is an open veranda under a roof. Everything is finished in wood, so it blends completely with the surrounding nature — including the long counter along the open side of the terrace, which takes the place of tables.

I chose a seat at the wooden counter, number 12, under a tall, spreading tree. The view from here is stunning! In the distance are the outlines of dark green mountains, closer are blooming trees and separate gazebos with light, sheer curtains.

There are also tables on the veranda, tables on the grass next to it and separate gazebos for 6–8 people — there is plenty of choice.

The first thing that sets the mood and the atmosphere is the music. Here it fits the style of the restaurant perfectly. Quiet and melodic, with Italian, French and English songs — a pleasant addition to a calm meal.

The menu

The restaurant has a main menu, a separate drinks list and a seasonal autumn menu from the chef.

It was the last one that caught my attention. There I found a set of 4 mini bruschettas for 90,000 VND. For comparison: one large bruschetta on the main menu costs the same. The waitress explained that the bruschettas in the set are small and the ones on the main menu are bigger — a nice touch of service. I happened to want to try 4 different flavours in one go, so I took the set as a starter.

90 000 ₫·$3.6·4 800 ₩

First I ordered a drink — classic kombucha (it is very popular in Nha Trang restaurants). Kombucha is the tea-fungus drink many of us have forgotten; remember, it used to sit in a three-litre jar in almost every home. It is a symbiosis of bacteria and yeast: tea, sugar and the tea fungus. I expected a glass, but got a whole 500 ml bottle. And the taste didn't disappoint — this is it, well-brewed kombucha. Delicious!

The set of 4 bruschettas arrived fairly quickly, on a small wooden board with a hollow, lined with paper. The base is thin Borodinsky rye bread with a generous amount of topping. It is piled high, not spread — and that matters! Here I can already give 10/10 for presentation and generosity.

1 — Bruschetta with ostrich pâté

A real discovery! It was my first time trying ostrich pâté. Incredibly tender and soft, it literally melts in your mouth. A piece of dried fruit on top adds a pleasant piquancy.

2 — Bruschetta with forshmak

Classic forshmak is made from herring, apple, butter and so on. In this bruschetta I missed a uniform texture. I could taste the slight bitterness of the herring, but the pieces were too large to blend together perfectly. In my view the forshmak could be improved, but the idea is good.

3 — Bruschetta with Napoletano sauce

This bruschetta delighted me. Perfectly chosen ingredients, perfectly executed: moderately spicy, moderately piquant, moderately salty. You can taste nuts in the sauce, which gives it extra depth. No single flavour dominated — the combination is ideal.

4 — Bruschetta with lightly salted salmon

A thin slice of Borodinsky bread, fresh green lettuce and lightly salted salmon — exactly as stated on the menu.

Overall the set was a great success — four flavours, beautiful presentation and an attractive price!

The main course

For the main course I ordered prawns in pineapple with cream sauce for 140,000 VND. I didn't choose this dish by chance: the restaurant's guests praise it especially in their reviews. And I can confirm straight away — yes, the reviews were right!

140 000 ₫·$5.6·7 400 ₩

Let's start with the presentation. On a large black plate lies half of a real fresh pineapple, with the prawns in sauce served inside it. In a small bowl there is fluffy white rice sprinkled with sesame, plus 2 slices of toasted bread. It looks like a work of art — 10/10.

But the main thing is the taste.

The prawns are cooked beautifully. They are tender, juicy and firm, fully cooked but not overdone. The pineapple pieces in the sauce are lightly stewed and soft, and with the cream sauce and prawns everything comes together harmoniously. Nothing superfluous, just the clean taste of pineapple and prawns. Bravo!

The portion size deserves a separate mention.

The pineapple half is quite deep, and there really are a lot of prawns and sauce inside. Together with the rice and bread it makes a very filling dish. I couldn't finish it.

Coffee and dessert

I ordered an americano. It came in a stylish ceramic cup on a wooden tray together with a glass of water. I have a criterion of my own: if a glass of water is brought with the coffee without being asked for, to me it is a sign of an attentive attitude to the Guest.

And the coffee itself was wonderful — aromatic and rich, and I am hard to please in this.

And what is lunch without dessert in such a beautiful place?

I ordered a chocolate brownie with a scoop of creamy ice cream and condensed milk. The presentation is lovely: a small square brownie with ice cream on top, decorated with condensed milk and served on a board tray. The portion is quite small, but after a hearty lunch it is enough. The brownie was served slightly warm and the ice cream cold, which made a pleasant combination.

But the dessert turned out to be the weakest part of my meal. The brownie was too hard and overdried, so dense that I could barely cut it with a dessert spoon. Besides, for my taste it was a little too bitter.

So my rating for the dessert is 7/10. The taste is pleasant, but the texture needs work.

Lunch cost me 395,000 VND = 15 dollars = 1,230 rubles.

395 000 ₫·$15·1 230 ₽·21 000 ₩

For this money I got not only lunch but also a rest in a pleasant place.

Service

The restaurant offers Russian and European cuisine. The waiters are a mix of Russians and locals. In the 3 hours I spent there, all the guests were Russian-speaking. The waiters are friendly and always ready to come over. The atmosphere is calm and relaxed; the vibe of this restaurant is like a trip to a summer house with friends.

Yes, it is far from the centre, but this remoteness is its great advantage! People come here not just for a bite but to relax: to have an unhurried lunch, meet friends, spend an evening with music in beautiful surroundings.

It is a wonderful place to celebrate birthdays or family occasions. On the grounds there are separate gazebos for groups of 6 to 10 people. By the way, while I was having lunch, a children's birthday party was going on right behind me. The parents pushed 2 tables together, and 8 children celebrated and played in the fresh air.

And in the evenings there are live music concerts here.

Summary

After my visit I gave top marks to the atmosphere, location, design, service, music, cleanliness and table setting. The food left an excellent impression; I especially liked the bruschettas and the delicious prawns with pineapple.

My main conclusion: Veranda is not just a restaurant but a little countryside getaway among mountains and greenery, with good food.

I RECOMMEND IT!
