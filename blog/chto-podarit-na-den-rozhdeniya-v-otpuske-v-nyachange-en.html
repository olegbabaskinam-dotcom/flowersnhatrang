5. Cakes and soft toys

What is a birthday without a cake with candles?

In addition to flowers, balloons and gift sets, you can order a cake from us. We work with a Vietnamese bakery and buy standard, familiar sponge cakes with cream filling for your celebration. Inside they can be quite simple, while on top they have complex, bright, unusual decoration.

Why do we choose these particular cakes?

The thing is, tastes in Asia are very distinctive, and local cakes may feel unusual to our tourists.

For example, a sponge cake with salted duck egg yolks and meat floss is popular here. It is topped with thin fibres of dried pork or chicken that look like yellow cotton wool. It also contains cheese and a special sauce. The result is an unusual combination of sweet sponge and a meaty taste.

Or jelly cakes. They are made of dense multicoloured jelly from agar-agar, coconut milk and various fruit juices. Instead of sponge layers there is solid jelly inside, while on the outside they can be decorated like ordinary cakes.

Among all the variety of Asian flavours, we find for you what is closest to what we know and love at home.

How cakes are made in Nha Trang.

Cakes here are made by small home bakeries that have a rule — never repeat a design, so every cake is one of a kind and no two are the same.

In Russia we are used to buying several identical cakes at once, but here everything is different. You have to come, look and choose from what is available. And what is available changes every day! Some cakes are made one a day, some are never repeated.

So when choosing a cake on the website, keep in mind that its decoration will differ slightly. We always try to pick a cake as close as possible to the one you chose. The cake is guaranteed to be a sponge cake with cream, but the decoration on top is up to the bakery.
