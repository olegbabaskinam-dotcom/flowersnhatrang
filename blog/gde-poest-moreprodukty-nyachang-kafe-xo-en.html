Where to eat seafood in Nha Trang: a local budget café in the south of the city.

Nha Trang is a small, cozy town by the sea, and of course everyone who comes here on holiday wants above all to enjoy the sea and the seafood.

The seafood here is varied: huge prawns, big crabs and small blue crabs, a wide range of shells, clams and other sea creatures, small fish, sea urchins — in short, the list could go on and on.

!!! I want to give a special warning to lovers of exotic food: be careful when ordering shark fin, crocodile meat or raw oysters / prawns in sauce — these are the dishes that most often cause food poisoning or indigestion, and you don't need that on holiday, so be careful!

There are different ways to try seafood:

1. Buffet with a fixed entry price

This is an “all-inclusive” café with a buffet. You pay at the entrance, on average from 250,000 VND = from 900 rubles. The price covers every dish on the common table and local beer (beer isn't included everywhere, you need to check). It's a great way to try a little of everything and get to know the local cuisine.

250 000 ₫·$10·900 ₽·13 300 ₩

2. Seafood restaurants

These are special seafood restaurants where everything is on display: huge aquariums stand in the hall with the food still swimming, and fresh fish, oysters and crabs with tied claws are laid out on ice.

Such restaurants are often open-air, so the fish can be grilled right before serving. Prices here are restaurant prices and depend on the location and how beautiful the place is. The more greenery, decor and decorations, and the closer to the sea, the more expensive the menu.

3. Local budget cafés

And then there are small, authentic local cafés located further from the city centre. They are not as noticeable or eye-catching and have no bright advertising, but in the evenings they are full: every table has a group enjoying cold beer and freshly cooked seafood.

Prices in local cafés are low and the food is very tasty! You just need to know which places to go to.

Yesterday I visited one of these local cafés in the south of the city, 5 minutes from the sea and the southern beach.
The café is called Quan an X.O
It is located at 17 Hoang Dieu.

From the sea, walk along the road past the Xavia hotel and further on — the place is opposite the Avari hotel. The café has been open for more than 7 years and in that time has earned a good reputation not only with locals but also with tourists.

It's open every day from 12:00, but during the day there's almost nobody there. In the evening, after 16:00–17:00, all the tables are taken. As soon as it gets dark, at 18:00, the café transforms: lanterns, fairy lights and signs light up, the grill outside crackles and spreads the smell of roasted meat, people arrive in groups, and instead of music the café fills with a quiet hum of voices mixed with the sound of passing cars. It creates a relaxed, easy-going holiday atmosphere.

The café has 2 floors. The first floor is open: you sit at your table while passers-by walk past. The second floor is cosier: there is a large corner table for 15–20 people for celebrations, and it is often taken by yet another group — I think it needs to be booked in advance. There is a smoking area with a bench, a toilet and a separate sink with a mirror to wash your hands.

The first floor has a more open atmosphere, the second is more private and secluded. There is a toilet on each floor.

We arrived at 19:45 and took the only free table on the 1st floor. The waiters react quickly: they immediately bring the menu and cutlery and switch on the fan above your table. There is a fan on the wall by every table.

The menu is varied: from salads and hot starters to Korean hot pot soup, which is served on a hot plate in a pot of broth where guests cook the vegetables and meat themselves. But what interested us on the menu was the seafood. We ordered 4 different dishes and local draught beer.

The waitresses immediately brought a big bucket of ice and 2 glass mugs. The thing is, in Vietnam it is customary to drink beer with a big piece of ice. It makes sense: the beer always stays cold, and the ice, slowly melting, dilutes the beer's strength, so you can drink a refreshing cool drink for a long time without getting drunk. And believe me, beer with ice goes down easier in the heat and refreshes better — try it yourself.

This café serves draught beer in 2-litre aluminium kegs; a keg costs 60,000 VND = 220 rubles. The beer is tasty and light and goes perfectly with seafood. They brought us a special spout for the keg to make pouring the beer easier.

60 000 ₫·$2.4·220 ₽·3 200 ₩

There is also a bar with spirits: bottles of vodka, rum, whisky (I think the X.O. in the name refers to whisky), and the fridge has Saigon, Tiger and Bivina beer — all the local brands. On the shelves there are 100 g bottles of cognac and a couple of bottles of wine.

The first dish brought to us was prawns steamed with spring onion and lemongrass. One portion has 8 large king-size prawns. They come with an unusual salty sauce for extra zest. The prawns were so hot that we burned our fingers trying to peel them. Gloves are served with the prawns.

A portion costs 100,000 VND = 365 rubles.

100 000 ₫·$4·365 ₽·5 300 ₩

The second dish arrived literally 3 minutes after the first. These are small fish fried in batter, served with tomato sauce. The fish are fried with the head on, and inside they have lots of roe — a very tasty, crunchy dish. One portion has 10 fish.

Price: 90,000 VND = 330 rubles

90 000 ₫·$3.6·330 ₽·4 800 ₩

The third dish came a minute later — oysters baked in cream with cheese. I consider this dish the house signature recipe! You must order it. One portion has 11–12 oysters. They are served piping hot, so give them a little time to cool. Pour a little spicy salty sauce into each oyster and eat this marvel. Tender, refined, they simply melt in your mouth. I recommend it!

Price per portion: 100,000 VND = 365 rubles.

100 000 ₫·$4·365 ₽·5 300 ₩

Next we were served grilled pork ribs. Don't expect them to look like the picture — long and thin; we were served small pieces of ribs, but that didn't take away their charm: the meat is soft with a smoky aroma because it was cooked on the grill, easy to bite and easy to chew. It's a kind of rib shashlik. Tasty.

Price per portion: 90,000 = 330 rubles

90 000 ₫·$3.6·330 ₽·4 800 ₩

Looking around, I realised that of the 10 tables on the first floor, only 1 was occupied by a group of Vietnamese; the rest were taken by tourists. The average visit lasts an hour to an hour and a half. Locals can sit in a café for hours discussing news and gossip, while tourists come only to have dinner and then go on with their walk. We sat for an hour and 15 minutes, and during that time 5 tables around us changed.

There are no desserts or sweets in Vietnamese cafés. In general, sweets “as we understand them” are a problem here: they don't eat chocolate or pastries, only fruit in various forms.

The service is good and there are plenty of staff: I counted 4 waitresses, a man at the bar, another at the grill and an older woman helping out. They serve quickly and with a smile and are sincerely happy to see guests, especially from Russia, since Vietnam has a particularly warm attitude towards Russia. The waitresses watch the guests, catch your eye and come over right away.

The atmosphere in the café is lively and unpretentious. We sat for more than an hour, ate 4 dishes and drank beer. We were so full that the last king prawn was left over — there was no room for it.

Dinner of 4 dishes for two with beer came to 440,000 VND. At the current exchange rate that's 1,650 rubles, so 825 rubles per person. The dinner included prawns, small fried fish, grilled ribs, oysters baked with cheese and 2 litres of fresh draught beer.

440 000 ₫·$17.6·1 650 ₽·23 400 ₩

We were very pleased and had a wonderful evening. The service is good, the staff are friendly, the products are fresh and it's very tasty.

I RECOMMEND this café.

If you live in the south of the city or are staying at a hotel in this area, come here for dinner in the evening after 17:00 — you'll love the lively atmosphere and the tasty authentic food.

Café name: Quán ăn X.O

District: South, close to the sea and the beach

Address: 17 Hoàng Diệu, Nha Trang

Cuisine: Vietnamese / Korean, seafood
