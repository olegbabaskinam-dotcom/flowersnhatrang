Nha Trang districts (Vietnam): where to live and where to stay.

Author: Oksana Yasnova · 19 November 2026

Translated from the Russian original written by the author.

A short overview of the city's districts from a local resident.

Nha Trang has five main districts: the Centre, the North, the South, the West and An Vien. But Nha Trang actually begins earlier — in Cam Ranh.

Cam Ranh — the resort zone.

The resort coast (Cam Ranh area, Khanh Hoa province) begins right after the airport and stretches towards the city for 30 kilometres. This zone is called Bai Dai.

The best five-star hotels line the sea here: Mövenpick, Alma, Radisson Blu and others. The hotel grounds are large, with pools, shops, water parks and their own beach. People come to Cam Ranh for an all-inclusive holiday. You can get to the city by taxi or by the hotel's free bus, which runs 2–3 times a day.

The Centre of Nha Trang

This is the most densely populated district, with the most hotels and apartments.

The Centre is the heart of all life in Nha Trang. Here you will find the famous white Lotus tower (Trầm Hương Tower), the main city beach with the red flag, all the main shopping centres and lots of hotels standing wall to wall, one after another. Concerts and festive shows take place on the promenade, and the City Hall stands right opposite the Lotus tower.

If you rent a flat or an apartment in the Centre to live in, keep in mind that it is noisy here. From 5 in the morning there is heavy traffic of cars and motorbikes, during the day there are crowds of tourists, and in the evening all the cafés and restaurants turn on music and the discos begin. Tourists drink, have fun and stay out until morning, so living and sleeping in the Centre is not peaceful.

The South of Nha Trang

Below the Centre, along the sea, lies the South of the city.

The district is quite large, with its own Binh Tan market for fruit and seafood and its own beach. It is an authentic Vietnamese residential area.

There are many private two- and three-storey houses, small shops and inexpensive, tasty cafés. People get up early here, at 4–5 in the morning, and go to bed early, at 8–9 in the evening, so evenings and nights are very quiet. The district is safe and calm, and good for long-term living.

The South also has hotels with sea views. They are not as luxurious as in the Centre and a little cheaper. There are quite a lot of apartments, mostly small five- or six-storey buildings tucked away deep in the residential area. Many Russian-speaking relocants live in the South, but they live so quietly and unnoticeably that it is as if they were not there at all.

An Vien — a district of luxury villas and seclusion.

An Vien is a separate district of Nha Trang, located on the shore of the bay at the southern foot of Chut mountain, not far from the cable car to Vinpearl island.

It is a district with sea views, where white stone villas are buried in greenery. It looks like an exclusive resort village and has the atmosphere of an expensive European resort closed off from prying eyes. There are several good boutique hotels in An Vien with fountains and golden decor.

But the district is not finished yet: there are no shops, only two or three cafés, and many villas and hotels are still under construction. An Vien is also inconvenient to get to, because it is on the outskirts of the city. But if you want seclusion and complete silence, with no people around — just you and a few neighbours — this district will be a wonderful choice.

The North of Nha Trang — the “Russian district”

The North of the city lies across the strait; the road there goes over one of the 2 bridges.

Right after the bridge begins the northern beach, the best one within the city limits.

Opposite the beach stand two large high-rise complexes: Oceanus and Scenia Bay. Oceanus consists of five huge high-rise buildings, and inside it there is a “Russian” neighbourhood with Russian shops and cafés. The North has a very close-knit and strong Russian community, so there is a lot of contact with fellow countrymen.

In the far North there is large-scale construction going on; I think in three or four years there will be a big new district with hotels, restaurants, parks and beaches.

The new district is home to the Vietnamese theatre Đó Theatre with the wonderful show Life Puppets. That is where I recommend everyone to go!

The West of Nha Trang — a district for everyday life

The West lies deep inside the city, towards the mountains.

There is no beachfront here and no such concentration of tourist hotels as in the Centre. Instead you can see the calm side of Nha Trang: ordinary city life.

The West is a large, lived-in district full of contrasts.

Closer to the Centre there are old Vietnamese neighbourhoods with small shops and cafés. Further on begin modern residential complexes and new private houses, which are often rented out to foreigners for the long term. Such houses are usually rented by large families who come to Nha Trang for a long time.

The West has large shopping centres — a significant difference from some other residential districts. Here you will find GO! Nha Trang, a big hypermarket and shopping complex, and Lotte Mart, a supermarket on 23 Tháng 10 street.

The West of Nha Trang is for ordinary, calm, everyday life. Here Nha Trang stops being a resort and becomes a city where people simply live.

Which district of Nha Trang to choose?

Nha Trang is a diverse city.

If you want to stay in five-star all-inclusive hotels with pools, beautiful grounds and good service, choose Cam Ranh.

If you want to walk around the city, go out, visit bars and restaurants and try different food in different cafés, that's the Centre of Nha Trang.

If you want quiet, calm and an unhurried life in a real Vietnamese district, choose the South.

If you want complete seclusion, with almost no people around, that's An Vien.

If you want to live close to the Russian-speaking community, buy familiar groceries and go to Russian cafés, choose the North.

And if you come as a big family, with children, parents, dogs and cats, and plan to stay in Nha Trang for a long time, take a closer look at the West of the city.

Nha Trang is different everywhere. And that is its main advantage: here you can choose not only a hotel or a flat, but also the way of life that suits you: be close to your own people in a familiar community / immerse yourself in purely Vietnamese life / or live secluded among greenery, enjoying the view of the azure sea every day.
